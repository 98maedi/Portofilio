Hi, I am Maedot Nahusenay a student, a dreamer, and someone who believes that learning is more than just grades, but a lifelong journey of curiosity, exploration, and self-expression. I love expressing myself through writing short stories, painting, and photography, and I’m always looking for ways to combine creativity with problem-solving to bring fresh perspectives to any challenge. Whether I’m working on a science project, designing a poster, or collaborating on a group assignment, I aim to bring originality, passion, and attention to detail to everything I do. I find inspiration in the world around me from nature and music to conversations with friends and I enjoy transforming those influences into meaningful work. This portfolio is a glimpse into my world the ideas I’ve explored, the challenges I’ve faced, the lessons I’ve learned, and the skills I’ve developed along the way, all of which reflect my growth as both a student and an individual.
